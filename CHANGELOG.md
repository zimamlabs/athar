# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- **First release.** Quick capture, Markdown notes (tags, collections, pin, favorite, archive, trash and restore), a daily journal with a month calendar, mood, earlier and later entry and a streak, desktop clipboard history, search across all three, writing insights, Home with your writing numbers.
- **Import from Thabit or Dawem.** Copies their `notes/` and `journal/` into Athar with every file checked, nothing overwritten and the old folder untouched.
- **English and Arabic**, right to left, Western or Arabic-Indic digits.
- **One Zimam folder:** `Zimam/Athar`, with the no-loss move from the private folder when All files access is allowed.
- First-run tour, one-time What's new, Light, Dark or System themes.
